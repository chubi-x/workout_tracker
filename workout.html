Kettlebell Back, Shoulders & Rotation

Two upper-body workouts focused on back, shoulders and rotation without meaningful squat, lunge or posterior-chain volume.

Frequency: 2 days per week

Duration: 25–35 minutes

Effort: Finish each set with about 3 good reps remaining.

Rest: 60–90 seconds between sets. Start with 2 sets for two weeks, then use 3.

Workout A — Back & shoulder strength

1. Supported one-arm row

8–12 / side
Upper backLats

Brace your free hand on a sturdy surface. Pull toward your hip and pause for two seconds at the top.

8–12 reps
LatsShoulders

3. Half-kneeling strict press

5–8 / side
ShouldersCore control

Squeeze the bell firmly, keep your ribs stacked over your pelvis and press without leaning sideways.

Workout B — Rotation & resilient shoulders

Complete the following as 2–3 controlled rounds. Accuracy matters more than speed.

1. Tall-kneeling halo

5 / direction
Shoulder mobilityUpper back

Circle close around your head while keeping your ribs down and neck relaxed.

Watch demonstrations ↗

2. Tall-kneeling chop

6–8 / side
RotationObliques

Move the bell diagonally across your body. Rotate through your upper back while keeping your hips controlled.

Watch demonstrations ↗

3. Half-kneeling rotational press

6 / side
ShouldersRotation

Start light. Rotate smoothly through your torso and finish with the bell controlled overhead—never force the range.

4. Low windmill

5–6 / side
RotationShoulder stability

Hold the bell in the lower hand. Hinge sideways with a long spine and watch the upper hand. Earn the overhead version later.

5. Figure-eight to hold

6 / direction
CoordinationRotation

Pass the bell behind one leg and receive it securely at chest height. Stay smooth rather than rushing.

20–30 sec / direction
ShouldersAnti-rotation

Progression

  • Weeks 1–2: two sets or rounds per workout.
  • Weeks 3–4: three sets or rounds if recovery remains good.
  • After that, add reps or weight—not both in the same week.
  • Reduce volume if LeoMoves performance, sleep, wrists or shoulders deteriorate.
Stop any movement that causes sharp pain, numbness or loss of control. This plan intentionally excludes swings, squats, lunges, deadlifts and high-volume cleans because LeoMoves already supplies substantial lower-body work.
